# Research checklist

For each provider researched, confirm you have answered:
- [ ] Free tier rate limits (RPM, RPD/RPH, TPM/TPD) — for the specific model(s) of interest
- [ ] Models available on free tier vs paid-only
- [ ] Features excluded from free tier (context caching, batch, flex, grounding, etc.)
- [ ] Free tier data policy — does Google/OpenAI/etc. train on your data?
- [ ] Commercial use explicitly permitted? Any regional restrictions?
- [ ] Cost of paid tier (per-token pricing for the model(s) of interest)
- [ ] Usage tier thresholds and what unlocks at each level
- [ ] Estimated monthly cost at the user's projected usage
- [ ] Recommendation: stay on free, upgrade to Tier 1, or go to a higher tier?
